How to Play

⛏️

1. Mine

Enter the cave and mine Mana Stones from rocks. Give them to the Cave Manager to earn daily mana.

🔮

2. Forge Spells

Draw any pattern on the Spell Canvas. The game reads your drawing and generates a unique spell from it!

⚔️

3. Battle

Equip your spells and fight other mages in real-time. Draw your spells to cast them!

💰

4. Trade

List your spells in the Marketplace. Buy spells from other players. Build your collection.

📈

5. Progress

Level up your Magic Circle (1-9 stars) to increase mana and unlock more spell slots.
